### 🌟 Featured Project

#### 📱 Nassif Phone — Multi-Vendor Marketplace & Smart Smartphone Ecosystem
A full-stack, cross-platform mobile and web ecosystem connecting mobile phone vendors with buyers. Features AI-powered specification comparisons, location-based store discovery, vendor management tools, and social engagement mechanics.

- **Technical & Feature Highlights:**
  - **🏪 Store, Booking & Sales Management:** Complete vendor management system allowing shop owners to track inventory, bookings, sales records, and link their social media profiles.
  - **🤖 AI Smart Phone Comparison:** Integrated AI assistance to deliver intelligent side-by-side hardware evaluation, pros/cons analysis, and multi-criteria specification filtering (price, chipset, camera, battery).
  - **🗺️ Geolocation & Distance Calculation (GPS / Leaflet / Google Maps):** Interactive map integration calculating precise distance between user location and nearby mobile stores.
  - **🔔 Social Follow Pipeline & Targeted Notifications:** Facebook-style store follow system. Users can follow specific stores to receive automated real-time push notifications (via FCM & Expo API) for new offers or updates.
  - **⭐ Rating & Review System:** Transparency engine allowing customers to rate and review local stores.
  - **📱 Unified Cross-Platform Architecture:** High-performance Next.js full-stack platform integrated into a React Native (Expo) app wrapper for web and native mobile deployment.
